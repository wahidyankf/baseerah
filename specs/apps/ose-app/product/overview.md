# ose-app — Product Overview

See [product/README.md](./README.md) for the full product framing.

## Summary

OSE Application performs AI-assisted gap analysis between regulator-published rule documents
and a company's internal policies. Target users: compliance officers and risk teams.

## Scope

- Regulatory document ingestion and storage
- Internal policy document ingestion and storage
- AI-assisted gap analysis between regulatory corpus and policy corpus
- Traceable GapItem records linking regulatory clause to missing policy area
